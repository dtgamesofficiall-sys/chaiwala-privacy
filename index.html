These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention

We do not store or share your personal data. Any data collected by third parties is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Children’s Privacy

ChaiWala is designed to be suitable for players aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn such data was collected, we will take steps to delete it.
